How we choose

Judged, not drawn.

A panel of Magnum representatives scores every entry against three equally weighted criteria. There is no element of chance in picking the winner.

33.3%

Originality

Is it a name we haven't heard? Something we could actually own.

33.3%

Relevance to the product

Does it say something true about a 30 g, lactose-free, four-source blend?

33.3%

Brand fit

Does it sound like Magnum, sitting next to everything else we make?

Highest combined score wins. Judges' decisions are final. In a tie, tied entrants answer a mathematical skill-testing question unaided, as required by Canadian law.

The fine print

Official contest
rules.

The summary below is for convenience. The full Official Rules govern the contest — [LINK TO FULL OFFICIAL RULES].

1 · Contest period

The Contest begins [START DATE] at [TIME] ET and ends [END DATE] at [TIME] ET. Entries received outside the Contest Period will not be accepted.

2 · Who can enter

Open only to legal residents of Canada, excluding Quebec, who have reached the age of majority in their province or territory of residence at the time of entry. Void in Quebec and where otherwise prohibited by law.

Employees, officers and directors of Magnum Nutraceuticals Inc. and its affiliates and agencies, and their immediate family or household members, are not eligible.

Limit one entry per person. Multiple entries using multiple email addresses or identities will be disqualified.

3 · How to enter

No purchase necessary. Submit a valid email address, verify it, open the unique link you're sent, and complete this entry form — a proposed name, your rationale, and the other required answers.

You must check the eligibility box before submitting. Entries without it are not accepted. Marketing opt-in is separate and has no effect on eligibility. You'll get a confirmation email on successful submission.

4 · Selection of winner

A panel of Magnum Nutraceuticals Inc. representatives judges entries on originality (33.3%), relevance to the product (33.3%) and brand fit (33.3%). Highest combined score wins, at the judges' sole discretion. Decisions are final.

This contest is a test of skill and judgment. No random draw or element of chance is used. Ties are broken by a mathematical skill-testing question answered without assistance.

5 · Prize

One (1) winner receives a one-year supply of Magnum Nutraceuticals Inc. protein powder — fourteen (14) two-pound tubs. The winner selects preferred flavour(s) and protein type(s) from the current line, subject to availability; unavailable selections are substituted with comparable products of equal or greater value.

Approximate total retail value $1,000 CAD. No cash value, not transferable or redeemable for cash. Magnum reserves the right to substitute a prize of equal or greater value.

6 · Winner notification

The potential winner is notified by email within three (3) business days of selection, at the address given at entry, and must respond within five (5) days, correctly answer the skill-testing question if applicable, and sign and return a Declaration and Intellectual Property Release Form to claim the prize.

If the potential winner can't be reached, doesn't respond in time, doesn't return the release, or doesn't meet an eligibility requirement, Magnum may select an alternate winner or not award the prize.

7 · Use of your submitted name

By submitting a name you grant Magnum Nutraceuticals Inc. a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, adapt, modify, register as a trademark and commercially exploit that name and any associated ideas, without further compensation, credit or approval.

You confirm your name is original, infringes no third party's rights, and is not obscene or otherwise inappropriate. Magnum may disqualify any entry that breaches this at its sole discretion, and is under no obligation to use any submitted name — including the winning one.

8 · Publicity and privacy

By accepting the prize the winner consents to Magnum using their name, city/province of residence and entry for advertising and promotional purposes related to this contest, in any media, without additional compensation, except where prohibited by law.

Personal information is used solely to administer the contest and, where separately consented to, for marketing — in line with Magnum's Privacy Policy, PIPEDA and provincial privacy law. You can withdraw marketing consent at any time.

9 · General conditions

Magnum may cancel, terminate, modify or suspend the contest if a virus, bug, unauthorized intervention, fraud or other cause beyond its control corrupts the administration, security or proper play of the contest, and may disqualify anyone tampering with the entry process.

Magnum is not responsible for lost, late, incomplete, illegible, misdirected or garbled entries, or for technical failures of any kind. By entering you agree to release and hold harmless Magnum Nutraceuticals Inc. and its officers, directors, employees and agents from any liability arising from participation or from acceptance or use of the prize.

This contest is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein.
